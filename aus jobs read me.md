# Australia Data Science Job Market (2019–2022)

An analysis of **3,902 data science job listings** from Seek (Australia), covering **Feb 2019 – Jan 2022**, built with **MySQL** and an interactive **Excel dashboard** (PivotTables, PivotCharts, slicers).

## KPIs
| KPI | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Job Listings | **3,902** |
| Unique Job Titles | **1,138** |
| Unique Advertisers | **923** |
| States / Territories | **10** |
| Cities | **36** |
| Suburbs | **186** |

## Key Insights

### Where the jobs are
- **Sydney (1,680) and Melbourne (1,045)** lead by a wide margin, followed by Brisbane (345), Canberra (315) and Perth (269).
- **New South Wales and Victoria** account for the bulk of demand across every dimension.

### Industries and roles
- **Information & Communication Technology** is the largest sector with **2,085 listings (over half the market)**, followed by Science & Technology (952), Government & Defence (208), Banking & Financial Services (165) and Consulting & Strategy (130).
- **Mathematics, Statistics & Information Sciences** is the top sub-classification (857 listings).

### Work type and hiring
- **Full-time roles dominate: 77% of listings**, with Contract/Temp at 22%. Part-time and casual roles are under 1%.
- This holds in **every state and every industry**.
- **Direct company postings beat recruiters** (2,457 vs 1,445, about 63% vs 37%) and lead in every state.
- **ICT (46.5%) and Banking (43.6%)** rely on recruiters the most, while Government (13.5%) and Education (1.2%) rarely do.

### Skills
- **Python is the most requested skill (3,501 listings, about 90%)**, followed by **SQL (2,895)** and **R (2,490)**, then Tableau, SAS and Excel.
- **Python demand is 87–94% in every major state** (WA 94%, QLD 91%, NSW 91%).
- **SQL demand reached 92% in 2021**, while **R declined from 77% in 2019 to 46% in 2022**.

### Salary transparency
- Only about **39% of listings mention a salary**.
- **ACT (45%) and NSW (43%)** are the most transparent states; **WA (26%)** is the lowest.
- **Government (63%) and Education (55%)** are far more transparent than the private sector, with **Marketing (19%)** the least.

### Employers
- Top advertisers are **Bluefin Resources (77), Correlate Resources (72), Talent Insights Group (62) and the Australian Bureau of Statistics (60)**.
- Employer rating does not predict hiring volume: the ABS (4.3 rating) posts 39 jobs, while La Trobe University and the Department of Home Affairs (3.1 rating) post 16 each.

### Hiring trends
- **2021 was the peak year (1,666 listings)**, with a clear **COVID dip in 2020 (806)** from 1,313 in 2019. The 2022 figure covers January only.
- **October (421), May (383) and September (373)** are the busiest months.
- **Postings are almost entirely a weekday activity:** Friday (788) and Tuesday (777) lead, while Saturday and Sunday see only 62 and 43.

## Dashboard
The Excel dashboard shows the KPI cards plus charts for Jobs by Year, Work Type, Classification, Top 5 Cities, Top 5 Advertisers, Salary Transparency, Top Skills, Day of Week and Top 5 Months, with slicers for State, Year, Work Type and Classification.
