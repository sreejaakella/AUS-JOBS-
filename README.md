Australia Data Science Job Market (2019–2022)
Dataset

3,902 data science job listings scraped from Seek Australia (Feb 2019 – Jan 2022). Each listing includes the job title, classification, advertiser, location (state, city, suburb), work type, salary, posting date and 24 skill flags (Python, SQL, R, Tableau, Spark, etc.).

Questions Explored

Location

Which states and cities have the most data science jobs?

Industry and roles

Which industries and job classifications hire the most?

Work type and hiring

What share of jobs are full-time, contract, part-time or casual?
Do companies hire directly or through recruiters, and does this vary by state and industry?
Who are the top advertisers, and does employer rating relate to hiring volume?

Skills

Which skills are most in demand overall and by state?
How has demand for Python, SQL and R changed over time?

Salary

How many listings disclose a salary, and how does this vary by state and industry?

Trends

How did job postings change from 2019 to 2022?
Which months and days of the week see the most postings?
